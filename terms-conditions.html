  1. Who We Are

 

Wellbeing Places Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a UK-registered company (Company No. 11342583) with its registered office at Dawson House, 5 Jewry Street, London, EC3N 2EX, United Kingdom. 

We operate consultancy, research, and wellbeing projects across the UK, including ESG, housing, and community development initiatives. 

We are the Data Controller for the personal data collected through our website and professional activities. 

For any privacy-related queries, contact: 
office@wellbeingplaces.co.uk

  1. Sharing Your Data

We only share personal data when necessary for business operations or compliance. Typical third-party processors may include: 

  • Microsoft 365 / SharePoint – secure email and document storage 
  • Google Analytics – website analytics (cookie consent required) 
  • Professional partners – for joint project delivery or research 

All third parties are subject to confidentiality and data processing agreements compliant with UK GDPR. 

We do not sell or rent personal data to any other organisation. 

 

  1. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes collected: 

  • Enquiries: up to 12 months after last contact 
  • Client/project records: typically 6 years post-project (for audit and compliance) 
  • Financial records: 6 years per HMRC requirements 

 

  1. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to: 

  • Access your personal data 
  • Rectify inaccuracies 
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”) 
  • Restrict or object to processing 
  • Data portability (where applicable) 
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where consent is the basis) 

 

To exercise your rights, contact office@wellbeingplaces.co.uk. 

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at www.ico.org.uk. 

 

  1. Data Security

We use secure, encrypted systems (Microsoft 365, SharePoint, and accredited UK servers) to protect your personal data from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access. 

Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only. 

 

  1. Changes to this Policy

We review our Privacy Policy annually. Updates will be published on http://www.wellbeingplaces.co.uk with a new revision date.
